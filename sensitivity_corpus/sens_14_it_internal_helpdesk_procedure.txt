INTERNAL — IT Helpdesk Standard Operating Procedure

Subject: Ticket Triage and Escalation Guidelines

All incoming helpdesk tickets should be triaged within 2 business hours
during standard support hours. Priority levels: P1 (system-wide outage)
— escalate to on-call engineer immediately; P2 (individual user blocked)
— respond within 4 hours; P3 (general request) — respond within 1
business day.

Tickets tagged "security" should always be escalated to the security
team lead regardless of stated priority. Use the internal ticketing
system's tagging feature consistently so reporting stays accurate.
