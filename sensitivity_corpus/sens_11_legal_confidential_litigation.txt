Litigation Exposure Assessment — Prepared for General Counsel

A former contractor has informally indicated intent to file a claim
alleging misclassification of employment status over an 18-month
engagement. Outside counsel estimates potential exposure, including back
pay and penalties, in the range of $180,000 to $340,000 if the claim
proceeds and is not favorably resolved.

No claim has been formally filed as of this writing, and this assessment
should not be characterized as an admission of liability. We recommend
proactively reviewing our contractor classification practices across
similar engagements before any claim becomes public. This assessment
should not be shared outside the General Counsel's office pending further
review.
