Meridian Holdings' Commitment to Regulatory Compliance

Meridian Holdings is committed to conducting business with the highest
standards of integrity and in full compliance with all applicable laws
and regulations in the jurisdictions where we operate.

Our compliance program includes regular employee training, an anonymous
ethics hotline, and periodic third-party audits of our business
practices. We publish an annual compliance summary as part of our
corporate responsibility reporting, available on our public website.

Questions about our compliance program can be directed to our public
affairs team.
