INTERNAL — Legal Department Process Memo

Subject: Updated Contract Review Turnaround Times

Effective immediately, all vendor contracts routed to Legal for review
will follow the updated SLA: standard contracts under $50,000 in annual
value, 3 business days; contracts over $50,000 or containing non-standard
liability terms, 7 business days.

Please submit contracts through the internal legal intake system, not by
email, so requests can be properly tracked. Rush requests require
director-level approval. This process applies to all departments
company-wide.
