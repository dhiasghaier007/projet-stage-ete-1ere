Introducing Real-Time Collaboration in the Meridian Platform

We're excited to announce that real-time collaboration is now live for
all Meridian Platform users. Teams can now co-edit documents
simultaneously, see live cursors from teammates, and get instant
notifications when someone comments on their work.

This feature has been in beta for the past two months with select
customers, and we're thrilled with the feedback. Real-time collaboration
is available today on all paid plans, with a limited version available
on the free tier.

Check out our documentation to get started, or reach out to our support
team with any questions.
