Meridian Holdings Launches Employee Wellness Initiative

Meridian Holdings is proud to announce the launch of its new company-wide
wellness program, effective next month. The initiative includes access to
an on-site fitness center, subsidized gym memberships at partner locations,
and monthly wellness workshops covering topics like nutrition and stress
management.

"We believe a healthy workforce is a happy and productive workforce," said
a company spokesperson. All full-time and part-time employees are eligible
to participate starting on their first day of employment.

More details will be shared during next week's all-hands meeting, and
employees can sign up through the employee portal.
