SEPARATION AND SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into between Meridian Holdings Inc. ("the
Company") and Daniel R. Ostrowski ("the Employee"), effective as of the
date of last signature below.

WHEREAS the Employee's employment with the Company is being terminated
effective immediately; and WHEREAS the parties wish to resolve, fully and
finally, any and all claims arising from the employment relationship;

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises herein: (1) the
Company shall pay the Employee a severance of $22,500, less applicable
withholdings; (2) the Employee releases the Company from any and all
claims, known or unknown; (3) both parties agree to keep the terms of
this Agreement strictly confidential.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement below.
