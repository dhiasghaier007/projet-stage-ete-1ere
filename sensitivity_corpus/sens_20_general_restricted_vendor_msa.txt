MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between
Meridian Holdings Inc. ("Client") and Vantage Consulting Group
("Provider"), governing the terms under which Provider will deliver
consulting services to Client.

1. SERVICES. Provider shall perform services as described in one or more
   Statements of Work executed under this Agreement.
2. FEES. Client shall pay Provider according to the fee schedule set
   forth in each applicable Statement of Work, net 30 days from invoice.
3. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the
   other's proprietary information disclosed in connection with this
   Agreement.
4. TERM AND TERMINATION. This Agreement shall remain in effect for two
   (2) years, renewable by mutual written agreement, and may be
   terminated by either party with 60 days' written notice.

Executed by authorized representatives of both parties as of the date
below.
