MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into between Meridian
Holdings Inc. and Northbridge Analytics LLC (each a "Party") in
connection with discussions regarding a potential business relationship.

1. Each Party may disclose certain confidential and proprietary
   information to the other Party in connection with the Purpose.
2. The Receiving Party shall hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential
   Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose it to any
   third party without prior written consent.
3. This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of three (3) years
   from the Effective Date.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have caused this Agreement to be
executed by their duly authorized representatives.
