Meridian Holdings Announces Fiscal Year 2026 Results

Meridian Holdings today reported financial results for the fiscal year
ended December 2026. Total revenue grew 12% year-over-year, driven by
strong performance across all business segments.

"We're pleased with our results this year and remain focused on
delivering long-term value to our customers and shareholders," said the
company's Chief Financial Officer in today's earnings call.

The full earnings report, including detailed financial statements, is
available on the company's investor relations website. Meridian will
host its next quarterly earnings call in three months.
