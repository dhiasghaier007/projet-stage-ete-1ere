INTERNAL MEMO — For Meridian Holdings staff distribution only

Subject: Updated New Hire Onboarding Checklist

To all hiring managers: effective this quarter, the onboarding checklist
has been updated. Please ensure the following steps are completed for
every new hire within their first week:

1. IT equipment request submitted via the internal helpdesk system
2. Building access badge issued by Facilities
3. Benefits enrollment session scheduled with HR
4. Assigned a peer buddy from within the team
5. First 1:1 with direct manager scheduled

Please route any questions to the HR Operations team. Do not share this
checklist externally as it references internal system names.
