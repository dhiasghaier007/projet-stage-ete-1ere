DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") supplements the Master Services
Agreement between Meridian Holdings Inc. ("Controller") and CloudVault
Systems Inc. ("Processor") and governs the Processor's handling of
personal data on the Controller's behalf.

1. The Processor shall process personal data only on documented
   instructions from the Controller, including with regard to transfers
   of personal data to a third country.
2. The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and
   organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to
   the risk, in accordance with Article 32 of the GDPR.
3. The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay after
   becoming aware of a personal data breach.

This Agreement is governed by the laws referenced in the Master Services
Agreement and executed by authorized representatives of both parties.
