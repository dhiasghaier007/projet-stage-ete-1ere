INTERNAL — Meridian Holdings Finance Team

Subject: FY2027 Budget Planning Kickoff

All department heads: it's time to begin the FY2027 budget planning
cycle. Please submit your preliminary budget requests through the
internal finance portal by the end of next month.

Standard guidance for this cycle: headcount growth requests require a
business justification memo; capital expenditure requests over $10,000
need VP approval; travel budgets should reflect a 5% reduction from last
year per company-wide cost discipline.

The Finance team will hold office hours every Tuesday for questions.
