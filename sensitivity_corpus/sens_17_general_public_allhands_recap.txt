Meridian Holdings Celebrates 10 Years in Business

This month marks Meridian Holdings' 10th anniversary, and we celebrated
with an all-hands gathering bringing together employees from all our
office locations. The event featured a look back at key company
milestones, recognition of long-tenured employees, and a keynote from
our CEO about the company's vision for the next decade.

Photos and highlights from the event are available on the company's
public social media channels. We're grateful to our employees, customers,
and partners for being part of this journey, and we're excited for what's
ahead.
