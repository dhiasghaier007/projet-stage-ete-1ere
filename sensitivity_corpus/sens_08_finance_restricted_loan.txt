CREDIT AGREEMENT

This Credit Agreement is entered into between Meridian Holdings Inc.
("Borrower") and First Continental Bank ("Lender"), governing a revolving
credit facility of up to $5,000,000.

1. FACILITY. Lender agrees to extend a revolving line of credit not to
   exceed the Facility Amount, subject to the terms herein.
2. INTEREST RATE. Advances shall bear interest at SOFR plus 2.25% per
   annum.
3. COVENANTS. Borrower shall maintain a minimum debt service coverage
   ratio of 1.25:1.00, tested quarterly.
4. EVENTS OF DEFAULT. Failure to maintain the covenants above shall
   constitute an Event of Default under this Agreement.

Executed by authorized representatives of both parties as of the date
below.
