HR POLICY: Remote Work Guidelines
===================================

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. OVERVIEW
All full-time employees are eligible for remote work arrangements subject to 
departmental requirements and manager approval.

2. REQUIREMENTS
- Employees must have a quiet, dedicated workspace
- High-speed internet connection required (minimum 25 Mbps download)
- Attendance at in-person meetings as scheduled

3. SCHEDULE
- Default: 3 days remote, 2 days in office per week
- May be adjusted based on role requirements
- Exceptions require executive approval

4. EQUIPMENT
- Company provides laptop and monitor
- VPN access required for all work activities
- Regular security updates mandatory

5. COMMUNICATION
- Core hours: 9 AM - 3 PM in employee's timezone
- Daily standup at 10 AM (virtual meeting)
- Availability on Slack required during business hours
